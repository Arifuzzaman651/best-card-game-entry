EMBER COURT

A duel of flame, sword and leaf. First to 0 life loses the Court.

  • Each turn: draw 2 cards, gain 3 energy. Play cards, then end your turn.
  • ▲ Flame burns. ◆ Sword strikes and draws. ● Leaf shields and mends.
  • Chains: Cinder Burst hits harder when a Flame card was already played this turn.
  • Shield soaks damage first and stays until it is spent.
  • The Warden plays in the open — watch it, hold a shield for its big turn, punish it after.

Keyboard: 1–5 play a card · Space ends the turn · R restarts
